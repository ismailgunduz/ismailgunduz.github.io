How I work with AI
01 · Product design

Research before pixels

I use AI to speed up competitor and user-behavior analysis: store reviews, rival flows and drop-off points become a short list of problems before I open Figma.

02 · Product design

I prototype in code

With Claude Code I turn flows into working prototypes and test them on real devices. BudgetSync, my own budgeting app, went from wireframes to a Flutter build this way.

03 · Performance marketing

Winning ads, rebuilt with AI

I break down the ads that already perform, then rebuild them with Seedance, Kling and Nano Banana and assemble every layer in After Effects: about 100 creatives a month.

04 · Performance marketing

One variable, real results

Each version changes one thing, such as the hook or the person, and is judged on trial starts and cost per trial, not clicks. 191 briefs across 10 apps since January 2026.
