MePic

AI photo and video app: one flow for dozens of effects, and the video ads that bring people in.

RoleSenior Product Designer
Timeline2025 – 2026
PlatformiOS · Android
CompanyOak Smart

Project summary & challenges

MePic turns a single photo into AI art, before-and-after edits and short videos: cartoon styles, aging, new hair, tattoos, figure packs, background removal, colour fixes and text-to-video. It has passed 5 million downloads on Google Play.

The challenge

  • New effects ship all the time, each with different inputs: one photo, two photos, a style, a prompt. They all had to feel like one app.
  • AI only works with the right photo. People need to get a good result on the first try, or they leave.
  • The result is the product and the ad: it has to be easy to compare, save and share.

My role

  • Designed the effect flows from upload to result, including tips, loading, empty and error states
  • Built the shared parts every effect reuses: style picker, prompt sheet, before/after result
  • Designed profile, settings and the Pro upgrade
  • Made AI-assisted performance video ads for MePic’s features

Built together with Oak Smart’s product, engineering and growth teams.

#01

One flow

The same four steps for every effect

Every effect follows one path: upload a photo, pick a style or write your own idea, create, then get a result to keep or share. The first time someone uploads, a short sheet shows which photos work best (one person, face and hands visible) so the first result is a good one.

Because the path never changes, a new effect only needs new styles and a new result, not a new flow.

Toonify, from upload to result

#02

Styles

One picker, many looks

Each effect shows a row of popular styles under the photo and a “See all” sheet with the full grid. The same sheet holds figure packs, filters, tattoos and singing faces, so people learn it once. A “Write. Create. Inspire.” sheet lets them type their own idea, with “Surprise me” for anyone who doesn’t know what to write.

The same sheet across effects

#03

Before / after

Results you can compare and share

The result screen puts the original and the new photo side by side with a slider, so the change is obvious at a glance. Under it: share, download, try a new photo or regenerate. A short line in the app’s voice (“Time Travel Complete!”, “Nice Ink!”) makes the moment feel finished.

Result screens

#04

AI editing

Brush, colour and background in the same frame

Generative AI tools need a little more control: a brush to mark what to remove, a colour picker with opacity, and a set of new backgrounds after the old one is gone. They live in bottom sheets over the photo, so people never lose sight of what they are editing.

Remove, recolour, replace

#05

Create Art

From a sentence to a video

Create Art starts from text instead of a photo: describe the idea, choose a style, turn sound on or off, and create. Video takes time, so the waiting screen says so and lets people go back to the app; MePic tells them when it’s ready. The result keeps the prompt, so they can regenerate or tweak it.

Text to video

#06

Account & states

Everything you made, in one place

Profile collects every creation, and each one can be opened, shared or deleted with a clear confirmation. Settings leads with the Pro upgrade, then the usual list. When the AI can’t do its job, for example when no face is found, the app says why and offers a way to try again.

Looking back

What worked, and what I’d try next

One shared flow made new effects quick to add and easy to learn. The cost is repetition: people upload the same photo again for every effect. Next I’d let them pick a photo once and try several effects on it, and test whether a short inline hint can replace the tips sheet.
